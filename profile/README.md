### LottoLedger
**The smart way to track and manage your lottery tickets.**

LottoLedger helps you generate and track lottery tickets for games like Powerball, Mega Millions, and Lotto Texas. Features include:

- 🎯 8 intelligent ticket generation strategies
- ✅ Automatic ticket matching & results tracking
- 📊 Detailed statistics and an odds calculator
- 🔓 Free to download with an optional Premium edition
